# Acceptance criteria C1–C11 (v1, 27 October 2026)

These criteria define "done" for this hackathon entry. They are written against the problem statement **Build for Your World** ("bring Meta's models into your domain; solve a problem you know firsthand; free up time and mental space; make something possible that wasn't before") and the judging rubric announced at kickoff: Impact 30 · Use of Meta AI tooling 25 · Technical depth 20 · Demo 25.

`measure/` prints C3, C5, C8 and cost; `tests/` cover C1, C6, C7; the rest are checked by hand before submission. Every number below is a **target** until a measured value replaces it in the README.

| # | Criterion | Why (rubric) | How it is checked |
|---|---|---|---|
| C1 | **Every model output produces an action or a card version** (a card version, a hide/warn action, an order draft, or a question back to the customer). No screen that only says "the AI recognised X". | Impact; agentic behaviour | `measure/` C1: every `product_seen` and every actionable `comment_labeled` has a consequence event (`caused_by`) within one cycle (≤ 5 s); `tests/` |
| C2 | **Three models on one shared timeline**, each with a measurable role and **provenance shown on the card** (which frame, which utterance, which comment). Muse Spark, Muse Voice Transcribe and SAM 3.1 are the core. Muse Image is out of P0. Muse Spark Open is considered only after the integration gate (30 Oct) if it shows a measured benefit. | Use of Meta AI tooling ("creative use beyond basic integration") | By hand: provenance visible in the console for each card version |
| C3 | **Temporal and contextual references are resolved:** "the pair just now", "the red one earlier", "same size as the person above" resolve to the right `card_id` by `ts_ms`. If unresolved, the assistant asks the customer; it never guesses. Target: `referenced_card_id` accuracy ≥ 0.9 on 50 reference comments. | Technical depth; Demo (the central scene) | `measure/` C3 |
| C4 | **Honest cards:** the card image is a SAM 3.1 cut-out, not retouched; the card price is the price the seller **said**, linked by `speech_event_id`; a seller edit creates a new card version in one tap. | Impact (buyer trust); Technical depth (provenance) | By hand on the replay and the live session |
| C5 | **Seller-relief metrics** on a 30-minute replay with 500 comments: order comments are not missed (recall of label `order` ≥ 0.9); seller interventions (card edits, approvals) ≤ 10 per hour; a phone number stays visible ≤ 5 s; 100 % of receipts carry the image and the price of the card version current at confirmation. Baseline for comparison: shops report four to five staff reading comments during a live (public source cited in the README). | Impact ("who benefits", "free up time") | `measure/` C5 |
| C6 | **Permissions stay in the order layer:** `orders/rules.py` decides do · ask · forbidden; no prompt can override it; invariant tests. **Every decision has a provenance log** (which comment, which card version, who approved and when) visible in the console. | Technical depth; autonomy with control | `tests/test_rules_invariants.py`; console decision log |
| C7 | **Deliberate fault handling:** a late comment (older `ts_ms` than the current card) still pins to the right card; a model reply that breaks the schema gets one retry, then `unresolved`, without stopping the stream; HTTP 429 → backoff; a quick product switch (< 3 s) retires the previous card instead of merging. | Technical depth ("holds up") | `tests/`; replay scenario in `data/sessions/` |
| C8 | **Published real latency:** p50 / p95 per stage (receive · wait · infer · display) in the README, produced by `measure/`. Card p50 ≤ 3 s and label p95 ≤ 1 s are targets; the published numbers are measurements. | Technical depth; Demo | `measure/` C8 |
| C9 | **Judges can run the demo in 5 minutes** from the replay and the simulators, at one stable URL from 3 November, without a Meta account. **One real level-2 live session** (the chosen Page, testers) is the main source of the video, with before/after numbers from that same session. | Demo; Impact | By hand |
| C10 | **The 3-minute video** opens with one sentence separating prior work (an earlier replay console, not shown) from what was built during the week; tells one story end to end (show the product → card → comment "the pair just now" → draft order → seller approves → receipt); includes one scene where the assistant is wrong (asks the customer, asks the seller); shows the README table for 3 s; uses the problem statement's own words ("your domain", "free up time", "possible that wasn't before"). | Demo; new-work rule | By hand |
| C11 | **"Why Facebook" is answered** in the README and in one sentence of the video, with sources: Facebook is the most popular platform for watching livestreams in Vietnam — 88 % of surveyed consumers had watched a livestream on Facebook and 77 % watch one at least weekly (Decision Lab, *Live Commerce Landscape in Vietnam*, September 2024, n = 700); the Vietnamese live-selling market across all platforms is reported at about 2.5 million sessions a month and more than 50,000 sellers (Báo Đầu tư, April 2024, citing AccessTrade). The RTMP input and the comment reader are replaceable (TikTok, YouTube later). | Impact | By hand |

**Out of P0** (decided before the scaffold, revisited at the 30 October gate): Muse Image; real Messenger with testers; MoMo/VNPay payments; post-live report and clips; text assistant; AI director; Muse Wonder.
